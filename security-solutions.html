Our 5-Stage Security Engineering Workflow

We follow strict engineering methodologies to ensure zero camera blindspots, reliable failover power, and aesthetic cable concealment.

01

On-Site Risk Survey

Detailed assessment of entry gates, boundary walls, critical vaults, and lighting to engineer optimum camera focal lengths.

02

Concealed Cabling

Industrial PVC conduit piping and pure copper Cat6 cabling with heat-shrink terminations, avoiding messy hanging wires.

03

Mounting & Calibrating

Precision mounting of dome, bullet, and PTZ cameras with field-of-view angle calibration and anti-tamper locking.

04

NVR & Network Config

Setup of PoE network switches, secure VLAN segregation, RAID storage initialization, and UPS backup power linking.

05

Training & Handover

Mobile app configuration for iOS & Android, admin training on footage backup, password encryption, and warranty sign-off.

Frequently Asked Questions: Security Systems

Answers to common security, installation, and monitoring questions from our clients in Islamabad & Rawalpindi.

Can I view my security cameras live on my phone while travelling abroad?

Yes, absolutely. We configure encrypted peer-to-peer cloud streaming via official apps (such as Hik-Connect or DMSS). As long as your phone has internet, you can view live feeds, playback archives, and receive instant intrusion alert notifications from anywhere in the world.

How do the cameras function during loadshedding or power outages?

Because Rahim & Sons is also an electrical & solar engineering firm, we seamlessly link your NVR and PoE switches to your solar backup system or a dedicated online pure sinewave UPS. Your security surveillance will continue recording uninterrupted 24/7 even during complete grid power blackouts.

How many days of recording history will be saved?

Recording capacity is customizable according to your security requirements. We utilize enterprise surveillance hard disks (Western Digital Purple or Seagate SkyHawk) configured with H.265+ smart video compression. Typical commercial projects retain between 30 and 90 continuous days before looping.

What warranty and after-sales support do you provide?

All our equipment comes with official 1 to 2-year replacement warranties. Additionally, we provide prompt on-site engineering support in Islamabad & Rawalpindi with emergency response within 24 hours.

Client FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions: Networking & E&M

Everything you need to know about cabling standards, fiber installations, and electrical switchgear in Pakistan.

Do you provide official Fluke test certification reports?

Yes, for every enterprise structured cabling project, each individual data drop is tested with industry-standard Fluke DSX CableAnalyzers for Return Loss, NEXT (Near-End Crosstalk), propagation delay, and resistance. We hand over the complete official pass/fail PDF report.

Can you clean up and reorganize our existing tangled server room?

Yes! Rack re-dressing and migration is one of our primary specializations. We work during scheduled off-peak maintenance hours (nights or weekends) to re-label, re-terminate, and install clean horizontal/vertical cable managers without disrupting your day-to-day office operations.

Why is proper electrical earthing vital for networking equipment?

Sensitive networking switches, fiber transceivers, and servers can be instantly fried by static discharge, power surges, or lightning. Rahim & Sons builds chemical earthing pits that maintain ground resistance below 1 Ohm, protecting hundreds of thousands of dollars of IT hardware.

What brands of switchgear and networking components do you install?

We work exclusively with genuine certified hardware: Cisco, Ubiquiti UniFi, MikroTik, Schneider Electric, ABB, Mitsubishi, 3M, and Schneider Actassi. We never use counterfeit or sub-standard copper-clad aluminum (CCA) cables.

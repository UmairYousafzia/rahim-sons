Turn Your Electric Meter into an Income Asset

Under NEPRA’s Alternative & Renewable Energy regulations, residential and commercial consumers with a 3-Phase connection can export surplus solar units to IESCO through an authorized bi-directional meter.

At Rahim & Sons Enterprises, we take 100% of the bureaucratic burden off your shoulders. From your initial application dossier, site inspection, and load sanctioning to the green meter installation and first net-metered bill verification, our engineers handle everything.

Requirements

Net-Metering Eligibility Checklist

Check whether your home or commercial building is ready for Net-Metering:

01

3-Phase Connection

You must have an active 3-Phase electricity connection. If you have single-phase, we handle the 3-phase load extension conversion with IESCO.

02

Sanctioned Load

Solar system size (kW) can be installed up to 1.5x of your sanctioned load. If load extension is required, we process it concurrently.

03

Three-Phase Inverter

The solar inverter must be a PEC/NEPRA approved On-Grid or Hybrid 3-phase inverter with anti-islanding protection.

04

Earthing Certificate

Separate AC and DC chemical earthing pits with resistance less than 2 ohms verified by certified earth-tester equipment.

05

Valid Ownership Papers

Copy of property allotment letter / registry, CNIC of connection holder, and recent paid electricity bills with zero arrears.

06

Turnkey Partner

Rahim & Sons Enterprises provides end-to-end representation from application to final green meter testing.
